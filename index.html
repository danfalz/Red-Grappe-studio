Services

What I take on.

Bring a clear idea, a reference board, or just a problem in your mod. We can define the right deliverable from there.

Ch

Character modeling & rigging

Custom humanoids and creatures with clean topology, UVs, materials, and deformation-ready rigs.

Ar

Armor & weapon assets

Wearable sets and props built to match the target game's scale, style, and technical limits.

Mo

Mod porting & retexturing

Move existing assets between formats, or give a familiar item a new material language.

Vf

VFX & scripting integration

Practical effects and implementation support for assets that need to behave in-game.

Av

Custom VRChat avatars

Performance-conscious avatars with expressions, toggles, and modular outfit options.

L2

Live2D rigging

Readable, responsive 2D rigs for streamers, characters, and visual-novel projects.

Process

How a commission runs.

The work stays visible at every stage. You get a clear scope before production begins and usable files at the end.

01

Inquiry

Send the idea, references, target game, and any hard requirements.

02

Quote & scope

We define the deliverables, timeline, revision points, and budget.

03

Concept & blockout

Shape and proportions get approved before detail work starts.

04

Production

Modeling, texturing, rigging, and implementation happen in focused passes.

05

Delivery & support

Files are packaged for the target platform, with notes for installation and updates.

◈

Commission with confidence. Verify that you own or have permission to use the references and source assets involved before commissioning any artist.
